Overview

About Steel Hangar & Aircraft Hangar

A steel structure hangar is the ideal building solution for aircraft storage, helicopter maintenance, aviation repair facilities, and industrial large-door applications. ZhongSai Steel Structure designs and manufactures custom steel hangars with clear-span widths up to 80 meters, accommodating aircraft of all sizes from small private planes to commercial jets and helicopters.

Our steel hangars feature large opening doors (sliding, hydraulic, or fabric), high clearances for aircraft tails, reinforced structures for wind and seismic loads, and optional insulation for climate control. We design every hangar based on your specific aircraft dimensions, door requirements, local climate, and site conditions.

Whether you need a single-aircraft private hangar, a multi-aircraft commercial hangar, a helicopter maintenance facility, or an industrial large-door workshop, we can deliver a cost-effective, durable steel hangar solution. We provide complete services from design to manufacturing and export, with installation guidance for your local construction team.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Steel Hangar & Aircraft Hangar

1. What size hangar do I need for my aircraft?

The hangar size depends on your aircraft wingspan, length, and tail height. As a general guide: small single-engine planes need span designed to project requirements, twin-engine planes need span designed to project requirements, small jets need span designed to project requirements, and commercial jets need 40-60m+ span. We recommend adding 3-5m clearance on each side and 1-2m at the tail. Send us your aircraft specifications and we will design the optimal hangar size.

2. What type of door is best for an aircraft hangar?

The best door type depends on your hangar size, budget, and usage frequency: Sliding doors are most common and cost-effective for hangars up to 40m wide; Hydraulic lift doors are ideal for frequent use and provide full opening; Bi-fold doors are good for medium-sized hangars; Fabric doors are a cost-effective option for temporary or low-budget applications. We will recommend the best door type based on your requirements.

3. Can you design a hangar with an office or living area?

Absolutely! We frequently design hangars with integrated office spaces, pilot lounges, conference rooms, workshops, parts storage, and even living quarters (hangar homes). We can design a mezzanine floor or a separate attached wing for these spaces. We will work with you to optimize the layout for both aircraft storage and your additional space needs.

4. How do you handle wind loads for airport hangars?

Airport sites are often exposed and subject to high winds. We design hangars with enhanced wind resistance: reinforced column bases, heavier steel sections, bracing systems, and wind-resistant door designs. We design based on your local wind speed data (up to Designed to project requirements for typhoon-prone areas) and can provide wind load calculations for local authority approval. We also recommend proper site orientation to minimize wind exposure.

5. What is the lifespan of a steel hangar?

A properly designed and maintained steel hangar has a structural lifespan of 50+ years. The steel frame is designed for 50-year structural life. With regular maintenance (repainting every 10-15 years, checking door mechanisms, gutter cleaning), the building will serve you for decades. The door systems typically last 20-30 years with proper maintenance and can be replaced independently of the steel frame.

6. Do you provide installation guidance for hangars?

Yes! We provide detailed erection drawings, step-by-step installation manuals, and video tutorials specifically for hangar construction (including door installation). We can also send our engineers to your site for supervision and training of your local installation team. However, we do NOT perform on-site installation ourselves. Hangars with large doors may require specialized door installation technicians, which we can help coordinate.
