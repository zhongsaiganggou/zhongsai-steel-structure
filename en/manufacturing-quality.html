01

Raw Material Inspection

原材料核验

All steel materials are verified with mill test certificates (MTC), visual inspection and dimensional checks upon arrival. Common grades include Q235B and Q355B (equivalent to ASTM A36 and A572 Grade 50). Special grades can be specified per project requirements. Each batch is traceable through material documentation.

Mill Test Certificate (MTC) verification
Q235B / Q355B standard grades
Visual and dimensional inspection
Full material traceability
02

CNC Cutting

切割下料

CNC laser cutting and multi-head plasma cutting equipment are used for precise cutting of steel plates and sections according to detailed drawings. 30kW laser cutting machines handle high-precision thin plate cutting, while multi-head plasma cutters handle medium and thick plate batch cutting. Nesting optimization maximizes material utilization.

30kW laser cutting machine
Multi-head plasma cutter
CNC nesting optimization
High-precision cutting
03

Assembly & Welding

组立焊接

H-beams and box columns are assembled using professional assembly machines, then main welds are applied with twin-wire submerged arc welding machines. Welding procedures follow project standards with certified welders. Critical welds undergo Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) and Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) to ensure quality.

H-beam assembly machine
Twin-wire submerged arc welding
WPS / PQR qualification
Certified welders
04

Straightening & Drilling

矫正钻孔

Welded members are straightened using 80-ton straightening machines to ensure flange flatness meets specification requirements. Gusset plates and connection plates are drilled with CNC drilling machines for precise hole positioning required for high-strength bolt connections. Dimensional re-inspection is performed after straightening and drilling.

80-ton straightening machine
CNC drilling machine
High-strength bolt hole precision
Post-process dimensional check
05

Custom Component Fabrication

异形构件加工

For complex components such as pipe trusses, box columns and custom connections, 8-axis CNC pipe cutting robots are used for precise cutting and bevel processing. Complex joints are fabricated with manual welding and specialized fixturing to ensure welding quality and dimensional accuracy. All custom components are managed by drawing-based numbering.

8-axis CNC pipe cutting robot
Pipe truss / box column fabrication
Complex joint manual welding
Specialized fixturing
06

Surface Treatment

表面处理

After welding, components enter the surface treatment process. Shot blasting is performed with 3.2m x 2.8m blasting machines to achieve Sa2.5 grade surface preparation. Epoxy zinc-rich primer and polyurethane finish coats are applied in a dedicated paint workshop. Hot-dip galvanizing or other corrosion protection options are available per project requirements.

3.2m x 2.8m shot blasting machine
Sa2.5 grade blast cleaning
Epoxy zinc-rich primer + polyurethane finish
Dedicated paint workshop
07

Quality Control

质量检验

Full-process quality control includes: raw material inspection, cutting dimensional check, welding visual inspection, non-destructive testing (UT/MT), member dimensional re-inspection, and coating thickness measurement. Quality hold points are set at critical stages, with inspection approval required before proceeding. Third-party inspection (SGS, BV, etc.) is supported per client request.

Full-process quality hold points
NDT: UT / MT weld inspection
Coating thickness measurement
Third-party inspection supported
08

Marking & Packing

编号包装

Inspected components are marked and labeled according to erection drawings for quick identification and positioning on site. Packing plans are developed based on member dimensions and transport requirements, including bundling, protection and packing lists. For export projects, container loading plans are provided to maximize container utilization and ensure transport safety.

Erection drawing-based member marking
Bundling and protection packing
Detailed packing list
Container loading plan
