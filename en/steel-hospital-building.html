FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to manufacture a prefab steel structure hospital building?+

Standard production takes 25-45 days depending on size and complexity. Rush orders available in 15-20 days.

Do you provide installation services?+

No, we do not perform on-site installation. We provide detailed installation drawings, bolt lists, video guides, and can send technical supervisors to train your local team. We provide installation technical guidance only.

What steel grades do you use?+

We primarily use Q355B (GB/T 1591) for main frames, equivalent to S355JR (EN 10025). Q235B for purlins and secondary members. Custom grades available on request.

Can you customize the design?+

Yes, every project is custom designed based on your dimensions, load requirements, local building codes, and aesthetic preferences. Send us your drawings or specifications for a free quote.
