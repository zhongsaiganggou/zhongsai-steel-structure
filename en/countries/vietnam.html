Vietnam

Climate & Design Considerations

Vietnam has a tropical monsoon climate with high humidity (75-85%) and distinct wet/dry seasons. Northern Vietnam (Hanoi, Haiphong) experiences typhoons from July-November with strong winds and heavy rain. Southern Vietnam (Ho Chi Minh City) has stable tropical weather with monsoon rains May-November. High humidity throughout the country requires robust corrosion protection.

💨 Wind Load & Seismic Design

Wind load design: 0.5-0.8 kN/m² (typhoon-prone northern areas 0.8-1.2 kN/m²). Seismic zone: low (Zone 1 per TCVN). Roof slope recommended 10-15 degrees for heavy monsoon rainfall. Additional bracing recommended for typhoon zones.

📋 Building Codes & Certifications

Vietnam National Technical Regulation on Construction (QCVN), Vietnam Standards (TCVN). ASEAN mutual recognition of ISO 9001. Steel grades: Q355B equivalent to TCVN 6486-1:2010 SS400/SM490. Hot-dip galvanizing per TCVN 8161:2009. ACFTA (ASEAN-China Free Trade Area) provides preferential tariff treatment.

Engineering

Recommended Steel & Corrosion Protection

Recommended steel: Q355B (GB/T 1591) for main frames, Q235B for purlins. Corrosion protection: Class C4 (high humidity nationwide) — hot-dip galvanizing 85μm + epoxy primer + polyurethane topcoat. For typhoon zones: thicker base metal (6-8mm minimum for columns) and additional bracing.

🔧 Installation Support & Local Construction

Vietnam has a well-developed steel construction industry with experienced local erection teams. ZhongSai provides detailed installation drawings, bolt lists, and video guides in Vietnamese/English.
