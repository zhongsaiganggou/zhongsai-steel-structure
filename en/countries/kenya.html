Kenya

Climate & Design Considerations

Kenya has a tropical savanna climate with coastal humidity and mild highland temperatures. Coastal areas (Mombasa) experience high humidity and salt spray, requiring enhanced corrosion protection. Inland areas (Nairobi) have moderate temperatures with two rainy seasons (March-May and October-December). Wind loads are moderate but coastal areas require higher wind resistance.

💨 Wind Load & Seismic Design

Wind load design: 0.4-0.6 kN/m² (coastal areas 0.6-0.8 kN/m²). Seismic zone: low to moderate (Zone 2-3 per Kenya Building Code). Roof slope recommended 5-10 degrees for tropical rainfall.

📋 Building Codes & Certifications

Building Code of Kenya (BCK), Kenya Bureau of Standards (KEBS). ISO 9001 quality certification accepted. Steel grades: Q355B equivalent to BS EN 10025 S355JR. Hot-dip galvanizing per BS EN ISO 1461 required for coastal projects.

Engineering

Recommended Steel & Corrosion Protection

Recommended steel: Q355B (GB/T 1591) for main frames, Q235B for purlins. Corrosion protection: Class C4 (coastal/industrial) — hot-dip galvanizing 85μm + epoxy primer + polyurethane topcoat. Class C3 (inland) — galvanized or painted finish.

🔧 Installation Support & Local Construction

Kenya has a growing construction industry with available local labor for steel erection. ZhongSai provides detailed installation drawings, bolt lists, and video guides. We can send 1-2 technical supervisors for 2-4 weeks to train local teams and oversee critical connections. We do NOT perform installation — we provide technical guidance and quality supervision.
