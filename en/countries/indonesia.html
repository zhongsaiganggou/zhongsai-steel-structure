Indonesia Climate & Design Considerations

Indonesia has a tropical rainforest climate with high humidity (80-90%) year-round, heavy rainfall (2,000-3,000mm annually), and frequent thunderstorms. Key design considerations: corrosion-resistant coatings for humid environments, steep roof pitches (≥15°) for rapid rainwater runoff, thermal insulation to control interior temperature, and proper ventilation to prevent condensation. Coastal areas (Jakarta, Surabaya) require additional salt-spray corrosion protection.

Wind Load & Seismic Design for Indonesia

Indonesia is located in the Pacific Ring of Fire, making seismic design critical. Basic wind speed: 25-30 m/s (varies by region). Seismic zone: high (SNI 1726-2019). We design all structures per SNI 1726 (seismic) and SNI 03-1727 (wind loads), with AISC 360 as reference. Key features: moment-resisting frames, proper bracing systems, base plate design for seismic forces, and flexible connections to absorb earthquake energy.

Indonesia Building Codes & Standards

Indonesia uses SNI (Standar Nasional Indonesia) for building design. Key standards: SNI 1726-2019 (seismic), SNI 03-1727-2013 (wind loads), SNI 03-1735-2011 (steel structure), SNI 03-6364-2014 (building administration). Steel grades: BJ37 (equivalent to Q235B), BJ41 (equivalent to Q355B). We provide full calculation packages in English for local engineer review and permit approval. ACFTA (ASEAN-China Free Trade Area) provides 0-5% preferential tariff for steel products from China with proper Certificate of Origin (Form E).

Major Ports & Shipping to Indonesia

Major ports: Tanjung Priok (Jakarta) — largest port, handles most general cargo; Tanjung Perak (Surabaya) — second largest, eastern Indonesia hub; Belawan (Medan) — Sumatra hub; Makassar — Sulawesi hub. Shipping from Shenzhen/Guangzhou to Jakarta: 5-8 days (direct service). To Surabaya: 7-10 days. Typical transit including customs clearance: 10-14 days total. We recommend Tanjung Priok for projects in Java, and Tanjung Perak for eastern Indonesia projects.

Recommended Steel Grades & Corrosion Protection

Primary steel: Q355B (equivalent to ASTM A572 Grade 50 / SNI BJ41) for main frames, Q235B for secondary members. Corrosion protection for Indonesia humid tropical climate: Shop primer: epoxy zinc-rich (80 microns). Intermediate coat: epoxy micaceous iron oxide (100 microns). Finish coat: polyurethane (60 microns). Total DFT: 240 microns. For coastal projects (within 5km of sea), add hot-dip galvanizing for secondary members and increase total DFT to 300 microns. All welds are shop-primed after fabrication.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the import duty for steel structures in Indonesia?

Under ACFTA (ASEAN-China Free Trade Area), steel structures from China enjoy 0-5% preferential tariff with a valid Certificate of Origin (Form E). Without Form E, MFN duty is 10-15%. VAT is 11% (standard rate in Indonesia). We recommend applying for Form E to maximize duty savings.

Do you provide SNI-standard calculations?

Yes. We provide complete structural calculation packages per SNI standards (SNI 1726 seismic, SNI 03-1727 wind, SNI 03-1735 steel) in English. A local Indonesian structural engineer (PII/IPB certified) will need to review and stamp the drawings for building permit approval. We work closely with local engineers to ensure smooth permit processing.

How do you handle corrosion in Indonesia humid climate?

We use a three-coat system: epoxy zinc-rich primer (80μm), epoxy micaceous iron oxide intermediate (100μm), and polyurethane finish (60μm), totaling 240μm DFT. For coastal projects, we add hot-dip galvanizing for secondary members and increase total DFT to 300μm. All materials are packed with desiccants and waterproof wrapping for ocean shipping.

What is the typical delivery time to Indonesia?

Total from order confirmation to materials on site: 6-9 weeks. Production: 4-6 weeks, shipping to Jakarta: 5-8 days, customs clearance: 3-5 days, inland transport: 2-5 days. For projects in eastern Indonesia (Surabaya, Makassar), add 2-3 days for additional shipping and transit.
