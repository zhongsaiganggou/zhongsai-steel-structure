About Agriculture & Poultry Farm

Steel structure poultry farms and agricultural buildings are the modern standard for livestock production, offering superior environment control, biosecurity, durability, and cost-effectiveness compared to traditional construction. ZhongSai Steel Structure designs and manufactures custom steel buildings for poultry, livestock, and agricultural operations worldwide.

Our poultry farm buildings can be fully equipped with automated environment control systems including ventilation, heating, cooling, lighting, and feeding systems. We design for optimal air quality, temperature uniformity, and biosecurity to maximize animal welfare and production output. We work with poultry equipment suppliers to integrate their systems into our building design.

Whether you need a 10,000-bird layer house, a 50,000-bird broiler house, a cattle barn, a pig farm, or a greenhouse, we can provide a complete steel structure solution. We export to Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Latin America, where modern livestock production is rapidly growing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Agriculture & Poultry Farm

1. What size poultry house do I need for 10,000 birds?

For 10,000 broiler chickens, a typical house is 12m wide x 80-100m long (about 1,000-1,200m²), providing 10-12 birds per square meter. For 10,000 layer hens in cages, the building can be smaller (about 600-800m²) depending on the cage system. We will design based on your specific equipment and stocking density.

2. How do you control temperature in poultry houses?

Our steel poultry houses are designed with insulation (sandwich panels), tunnel ventilation (exhaust fans + air inlets), evaporative cooling pads for hot climates, and heating systems for cold climates. An automated environment controller monitors temperature and humidity and adjusts the systems accordingly. We design based on your local climate extremes.

3. Can you integrate poultry equipment into the building?

We work with major poultry equipment suppliers and can design the building structure to accommodate their systems. We will need your equipment layout drawings (cages, feeders, drinkers, fans, heaters) to ensure proper column placement, electrical wiring, and structural support. We can also recommend equipment suppliers if needed.

4. What is the lifespan of a steel poultry house?

A properly designed and maintained steel poultry house has a structural lifespan of 30-50 years. The steel frame is designed for 50-year structural life. The insulation panels and cladding typically last 20-30 years. Regular maintenance (cleaning, checking for corrosion, repainting) will extend the life. The corrosive environment of poultry houses requires proper surface treatment (hot-dip galvanized or epoxy coating).

5. How much does a steel poultry farm cost?

The cost depends on size, insulation type, ventilation system, and equipment level. A basic steel frame + cladding poultry house costs $30-60 per square meter. A fully equipped house (insulation, ventilation, heating, feeding) can cost $80-150 per square meter. Contact us for a detailed free quote based on your specific requirements.

6. Do you provide installation guidance for poultry farms?

Yes! We provide detailed erection drawings, step-by-step installation manuals, and video tutorials. We can also send our engineers to your site for supervision and training of your local installation team. However, we do NOT perform on-site installation ourselves. We recommend hiring a local construction team familiar with steel structure erection.
