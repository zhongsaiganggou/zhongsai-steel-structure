Residential Light Steel Structure System

Light steel villas, apartment buildings, rural new houses. Cold-formed thin-walled light steel keel system, earthquake and wind resistant, energy efficient, fast construction, high space utilization, suitable for private residences, vacation homes, apartments.

Product Overview

ZhongSai Steel Structure industrial building system uses prefabricated design. Main load-bearing components (steel columns, beams, purlins) are prefabricated in our factory, and only bolted connections are needed on site, significantly shortening construction time.

The system supports single-span, multi-span, with crane, with mezzanine layouts, and can be custom-designed according to your production process requirements. Widely used in machinery manufacturing, auto parts, electronics, food processing, logistics warehousing and other industries.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Residential Light Steel buildings

How much does an Residential Light Steel building cost?+

The cost varies based on span, eave height, crane configuration, and cladding system. Generally $45-$120 per square meter for the steel frame only. Exact price requires a detailed quote based on your project drawings. We provide free quotes within 24 hours.

Can steel workshops be equipped with overhead cranes?+

Yes. Our system supports 5-50 ton cranes including single-girder, double-girder, and bridge cranes. We design the structure according to your production requirements to ensure load-bearing safety.

What is the production lead time?+

Standard industrial buildings take 25-45 days. Small warehouses can be completed in 2-3 weeks, large plants may need 6-8 weeks. Rush orders can be coordinated for shipment within 7 days.

Do you provide installation services?+

We do not perform on-site construction, but provide comprehensive installation support: detailed drawings, 24/7 remote video support, and for complex projects, engineers can be dispatched overseas to guide installation and train local teams.

What is the lifespan of a steel building?+

Under normal use and maintenance, 50+ years. We use Q355B steel with epoxy zinc-rich primer + polyurethane topcoat. Regular maintenance (re-coating every 5-10 years) further extends lifespan.
