Project Case

Brazil Chinese Supermarket Steel Structure Project

2,800 m² Prefab Steel Supermarket in Sao Paulo, Brazil | 180 Tons Steel | 30-Day Production

Project Overview

This project is located in Sao Paulo, Brazil's largest city, and is a comprehensive supermarket in a Chinese community. The client is a Chinese businessman who has been operating in Brazil for many years, who contacted ZhongSai Steel Structure through a friend's recommendation. After 1 month of technical communication and factory video inspection, the contract was finally signed.

The project adopts a single-span steel frame structure with 24m span, 117m length, and 8m eave height. The first floor is the supermarket sales area (2,200 m²), with a 2-story steel structure mezzanine office area (600 m²) at one end, including offices, meeting rooms, employee lounge, and storage area. The front uses glass curtain wall, and the sides use sandwich panels. The main steel structure was shipped to Santos Port via 3 x 40HQ containers. The client organized installation independently, and we dispatched an engineer for on-site guidance for 10 days.

Common questions about this project

What are the advantages of steel structure supermarkets over traditional buildings?+

Advantages: 1) Large-span column-free space, 15-30m span, flexible shelf layout; 2) Fast construction, main installation in 4-6 weeks, 60% faster than traditional buildings; 3) Mezzanine can be added to utilize height space; 4) Modern appearance, compatible with glass curtain wall; 5) Recyclable, 100% steel recycling; 6) Low comprehensive cost, short construction period, low foundation cost.

What are Brazil's building codes and certification requirements?+

Brazilian steel structures follow ABNT standards: NBR 8800 steel structure design code, NR-18 safety code, INMETRO certification. Our design meets GB and ASTM standards simultaneously, adjustable to ABNT. Export requires: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (China-Brazil FTA tariff preference), Mill Test Certificate.

How to design the mezzanine office area for a steel structure supermarket?+

Mezzanine office design: 1) Located at one end or both sides of the supermarket; 2) Ground floor clear height 4-5m, mezzanine clear height 2.8-3m; 3) Steel frame + profiled steel plate composite floor; 4) Office live load 2.0 kN/m², storage 3.0-5.0 kN/m²; 5) At least 2 evacuation stairs; 6) Fire compartments, sprinkler system, emergency lighting.

What are the special requirements for steel structure export to the South American market?+

South America export requirements: 1) Language: Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish (others); 2) Standards: ABNT, IRAM, INN, etc.; 3) Certification: INMETRO, IRAM, etc.; 4) Tariffs: 10-35%, FTA preferences available; 5) Transportation: 30-45 days sea freight, good rust prevention; 6) Payment: strict foreign exchange control, recommend letter of credit; 7) Customs clearance: complex process, recommend professional broker.
