Project Case

Nigeria Logistics Warehouse Steel Structure Project

6,800 m² Prefab Steel Logistics Warehouse in Lagos, Nigeria | 520 Tons Steel | 40-Day Production

Project Overview

This project is located in Lagos, Nigeria's largest city, and is a modern logistics distribution warehouse. The client is a well-known local logistics enterprise in Nigeria, who found ZhongSai Steel Structure through Google search. After 2 months of technical communication and factory video inspection, the contract was finally signed.

The project adopts a double-span portal frame structure, each span 24m, total width 48m, length 142m, eave height 10m. Equipped with 8 hydraulic loading docks, the roof uses natural ventilators and skylight strips, and the walls use sandwich panels for insulation. The main steel structure was shipped to Apapa Port, Lagos via 6 x 40HQ containers. The client organized installation independently, and we provided remote video guidance.

Common questions about this project

What documents are required to export steel structures to Nigeria?+

Exporting steel structures to Nigeria typically requires: 1) Commercial Invoice; 2) Packing List; 3) Bill of Lading; 4) Certificate of Origin; 5) Mill Test Certificate; 6) SONCAP certification. We provide a complete set of export documents and assist clients with SONCAP certification to ensure smooth customs clearance.

How does Nigeria's climate affect steel structures?+

Nigeria has a tropical savanna climate with high temperature and humidity, and severe salt spray corrosion in coastal areas. We use C4-grade anti-corrosion coating, hot-dip galvanizing for key components in coastal projects, high-slope roof design for drainage, and enhanced ventilation to reduce indoor humidity, ensuring 15+ years anti-corrosion lifespan.

Why do logistics warehouses need high eave height design?+

Logistics warehouses need high eave height for: 1) Pallet racking storage requiring 8-12m clear height; 2) High-reach forklifts needing sufficient operating height; 3) High space facilitating ventilation and heat dissipation; 4) Easy to add mezzanines or racks later; 5) Loading docks matching truck height. Our logistics warehouses typically have 8-12m eave height.

How do you ensure structural safety for long-span warehouses?+

We ensure structural safety through: 1) Professional structural engineers using PKPM, SAP2000 software for precise calculations; 2) Strict design according to GB 50017, ASTM AISC standards; 3) Considering multiple load combinations including wind, snow, seismic, and crane loads; 4) Reinforced design for key connections; 5) Overall pre-assembly before shipment; 6) Detailed installation drawings and technical support.
