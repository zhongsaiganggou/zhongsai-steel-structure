Project Overview

A modern high-rack logistics warehouse in Lagos, Nigeria, with 10-meter eave height, insulated sandwich panels, and 6 loading docks. The project was designed for high-density storage with racking integration and fast turnaround for e-commerce and distribution operations. ZhongSai provided complete design, manufacturing, SONCAP certification, and export to Apapa Port, Lagos.

Frequently Asked Questions

Was SONCAP certification provided?

Yes. ZhongSai provided both the Product Certificate (PC, valid 1 year) and per-shipment SONCAP Certificate (SC) as part of our standard service for Nigerian exports.

How was the high-rack warehouse designed?

The 10-meter eave height was designed with 250mm x 250mm square columns, reinforced base plates, and racking-anchored floor design. The roof used 100mm insulated sandwich panels for temperature control in Lagos tropical climate.

What about port congestion in Lagos?

We advised the client to use Tin Can Island Port as an alternative when Apapa was congested, and pre-cleared customs documentation before ship arrival to minimize demurrage charges. Total port-to-site time was 10-14 days.

Did the client need a local engineer?

Yes. For building permit approval in Nigeria, a COREN-registered local structural engineer was required to review and stamp the drawings. ZhongSai provided complete calculation packages to facilitate this review.
