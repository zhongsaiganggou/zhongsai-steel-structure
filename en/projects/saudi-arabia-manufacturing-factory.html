Frequently Asked Questions

Was SASO certification included?

Yes. ZhongSai is SABER-registered and provided both Product Certificate (PC) and per-shipment SONCAP Certificate (SC) as part of the quotation, with no extra cost to the client.

How was the 20-ton crane building designed?

The crane runway beams were designed per AISC specifications with Q355B steel, including deflection limits of L/1000 for crane girders and proper bracing for lateral loads during crane operation.

What about the extreme heat in Saudi Arabia?

High-temperature resistant coatings (up to 120°C) were specified for roof purlins, and thermal insulation sandwich panels (100mm thick) were used to maintain interior temperature. Expansion joints were designed for 50°C temperature variation.

How long did SASO certification take?

The Product Certificate (PC) was obtained in 2 weeks before production. The per-shipment SONCAP Certificate (SC) was issued within 3-5 days after shipment details were confirmed.
