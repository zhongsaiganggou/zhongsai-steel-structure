5,Available upon request Prefab Steel Manufacturing Factory in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia | Available upon request Steel | 35-Day Production

Project Overview

This project is located in Riyadh, the capital of Saudi Arabia, and is a modern manufacturing factory integrating machining, assembly, and warehousing. The client is a well-known local machinery equipment manufacturer in Saudi Arabia, who contacted ZhongSai Steel Structure through the Made-in-China platform. After 4 rounds of technical communication and quotations, they finally chose us as the steel structure supplier.

The project adopts a single-span portal frame structure with 30m span, 183m length, and 12m eave height. Equipped with 2 x 20-ton double-girder cranes, and a 3-story steel structure mezzanine office area (about Available upon request) at one end of the factory. The roof uses high-reflectivity color-coated panels and 100mm glass wool insulation, and the walls use sandwich panels. The main steel structure was shipped to Jeddah Port via 5 x 40HQ containers. The client organized installation independently, and we dispatched an engineer for on-site guidance for 15 days.

Common questions about this project

What certifications are required to export steel structures to Saudi Arabia?+

Exporting steel structures to Saudi Arabia typically requires: 1) Commercial Invoice; 2) Packing List; 3) Bill of Lading; 4) Certificate of Origin; 5) Mill Test Certificate; 6) SASO certification. We provide a complete set of export documents and assist clients with SASO certification to ensure smooth customs clearance.

How does Saudi Arabia's high temperature climate affect steel structures?+

Saudi Arabia summer temperatures can reach 50°C+, with large day-night temperature differences and strong UV radiation. We use high-temperature resistant anti-corrosion coating, high-reflectivity color-coated panels, 100mm+ glass wool insulation, enhanced ventilation systems, and high-temperature resistant sealants to ensure structural safety and comfort in extreme high-temperature environments.

Why do manufacturing factories need mezzanine office areas?+

Manufacturing factories need mezzanine office areas for: 1) Fully utilizing factory height without occupying production area; 2) Separating office from production to reduce noise and dust; 3) Management can overlook the production floor; 4) Steel structure mezzanine is fast to construct and can be added later; 5) Saving land costs and improving space utilization.

What are the special structural design requirements for a 20-ton crane factory?+

Special requirements for a 20-ton crane factory: 1) Variable cross-section column design, reinforced at crane beam level; 2) Crane beams use welded H-beams with fatigue verification; 3) Enhanced column bracing to transfer horizontal forces; 4) Reinforced roof system to ensure rigidity; 5) Crane rails connected with special clamping plates; 6) Anti-collision corner protectors on columns in crane operating areas.
