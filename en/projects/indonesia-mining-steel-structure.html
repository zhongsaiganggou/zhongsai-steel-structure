Project Overview

ZhongSai Steel Structure supplied a complete heavy-duty steel structure for a mineral processing plant in Jakarta, Indonesia. The project included equipment platforms, crane systems, anti-corrosion coatings, and specialized structural design for heavy mining machinery. All materials were manufactured in our Dongguan and Huizhou factories, container-loaded, and exported to Indonesia with full technical documentation for local installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was the total project timeline?

From order confirmation to materials on site: approximately 10-12 weeks (35 days production + 12-15 days shipping + customs clearance).

Did ZhongSai provide on-site installation?

No. ZhongSai provides design, manufacturing, and export only. Installation was completed by the client local construction team with our detailed drawings and remote technical support.

What certifications were required for Indonesia?

Indonesia accepts GB, ASTM, and EN standards. Material test reports (MTR) and manufacturer declaration of conformity were provided. SNI certification was handled by the local importer.

How was the heavy equipment platform designed?

The equipment platform was designed for 5-ton/m² live load with Q355B steel, including vibration analysis for mining machinery and anti-corrosion coating for coastal environment.
