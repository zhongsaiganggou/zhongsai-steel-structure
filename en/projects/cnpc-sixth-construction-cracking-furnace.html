Project Overview

The CNPC Sixth Construction Cracking Furnace Project is a large petrochemical cracking furnace steel structure project. It includes the cracking furnace main frame, equipment platform and supporting systems. The project uses approximately 6,000 tons of steel.

ZhongSai Steel Structure participated in the manufacturing and supply of steel structural components for this project, including the cracking furnace main frame, equipment platform, supporting systems and connecting components. The project uses approximately 6,000 tons of steel, and all components are factory prefabricated according to design drawings.
