Project Overview

The Guangzhou Gongkong Greater Bay Area High-end Equipment R&D and Production Base Project is located in Nansha District, Guangzhou City, Guangdong Province. It is a large high-end equipment R&D and production complex project. The project has a building height of 42 meters and a span of 60 meters.

ZhongSai Steel Structure participated in the manufacturing and supply of steel structural components for this project, including main structural steel members, secondary structural systems, and enclosure system supporting materials. The project uses approximately 9,800 tons of steel, and all components are factory prefabricated according to design drawings.
