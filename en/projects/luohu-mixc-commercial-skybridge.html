Project Overview

The Luohu MixC Commercial Center Skybridge Project is located in Luohu District, Shenzhen City, Guangdong Province. It is a steel structure pedestrian skybridge project connecting various buildings of the MixC commercial center. The project uses approximately 500 tons of steel.

ZhongSai Steel Structure participated in the manufacturing and supply of steel structural components for this project, including the skybridge main truss, supporting systems, connecting components and enclosure system. The project uses approximately 500 tons of steel, and all components are factory prefabricated according to design drawings.
