Frequently Asked Questions

What certifications are required for industrial warehouses in Dubai?

Industrial buildings in Dubai typically require DM (Dubai Municipality) approved drawings and construction permits, with steel required to meet ASTM or BS standards. ZhongSai provides material certificates compliant with ASTM A36/A992 standards, factory inspection reports, and welding procedure specifications (WPS/PQR) to assist clients with the DM approval process.

How is insulation designed for extreme high temperatures?

Dubai summer temperatures can reach above 50°C. This project uses 100mm thick polyurethane insulated sandwich panels (PIR) with thermal conductivity ≤0.022W/m·K, and a ventilated air gap layer on the roof to reduce solar radiation heat. Light-colored coatings on walls reflect sunlight, combined with ridge ventilators and louvers to achieve natural ventilation cooling.

How is wind and sand resistance designed for desert areas?

Dubai basic wind pressure is designed at 0.75kN/m², classified as a strong wind zone. Doors and windows use double-layer sealing strips, waterproof breathable membranes are added at roof laps, and sand baffles are designed at eaves. Steel structure surfaces use a three-coat anti-corrosion system of zinc-rich epoxy primer + polyurethane topcoat, with salt spray corrosion resistance ≥1000 hours, adapting to the high-salt, high-humidity environment of the Gulf region.

How long does customs clearance take at Jebel Ali Port?

Sea freight from Shenzhen to Jebel Ali Port takes approximately 18-22 days, and customs clearance after arrival typically takes 3-7 days. Dubai has efficient customs clearance. We recommend clients prepare import permits and EWR (Electronic Waybill Receipt) in advance. ZhongSai provides complete packing lists, commercial invoices, certificates of origin (CO), and material certificates to ensure fast customs clearance.
