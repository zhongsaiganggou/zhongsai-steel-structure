Steel Structure Product FAQs

Frequently asked questions about sourcing steel structure products from ZhongSai

What steel structure products does ZhongSai offer?+

ZhongSai Steel Structure offers 6 steel structure product systems: Industrial Building Steel Structure (factories, warehouses, workshops, assembly plants), Mining & Energy Heavy Steel Structure (mining plants, conveyor corridors, smelters, power station pipe racks), Commercial & Public Building Steel Structure (supermarkets, car dealerships, offices, hotels, exhibition halls, stadiums, schools, hospitals), Residential Light Steel Structure (villas, apartments, homes), Agriculture & Special Steel Structure (livestock farms, equipment platforms, parking sheds), Custom Steel Structure Engineering (bridges, corridors, hangars, offshore platforms, custom components).

What steel grades do you use?+

We primarily use Q235B and Q355B (equivalent to ASTM A36 and A572 Grade 50) for main framing. For projects requiring higher strength or corrosion resistance, we can specify ASTM A992, weathering steel, or galvanized components. All materials come with Mill Test Certificates (MTC EN 10204 3.1).

Can you design for our local wind and snow loads?+

Absolutely. Our engineering team designs according to your local building codes and site-specific loads. We regularly design for high wind zones (hurricane/typhoon zones up to 150 mph), heavy snow zones (snow loads up to 150 kg/m²), seismic zones (up to magnitude 8+), and extreme temperature ranges.

Do you provide installation services?+

We do not perform on-site construction installation, but we provide comprehensive installation support at three levels: (1) Detailed installation drawings with numbered components, (2) 24/7 remote technical support via WhatsApp video, (3) For complex or large projects, we can dispatch engineers to supervise installation and train your local construction team.

What is the typical production lead time?+

Standard project production lead time is 25-45 days, depending on project size and complexity. Small warehouses can be completed in 2-3 weeks, while large industrial factories may require 6-8 weeks. Rush orders can be coordinated in 7 days. We clearly specify delivery time in the contract.

How is the export packaging done?+

We use professional export packaging: steel components are bundled in installation sequence with rust-proof paint and waterproof membrane double protection, long components are fixed with special steel frames, bolt accessories are packed separately with detailed lists. Each container comes with a detailed packing list and component location diagram. We export 700+ containers annually with extensive packaging experience.
