About ZhongSai Steel Structure — China Steel Structure Manufacturer & Exporter

15+ years of excellence in steel structure design, manufacturing, and global export. Your trusted full-service partner from China — design, manufacturing, export and installation guidance only.

Our Story

15+ Years of Steel Structure Excellence

Shenzhen ZhongSai Steel Structure Import and Export Co., Ltd. is a large-scale steel structure enterprise integrating design, production, processing, export, and overseas technical guidance. With 15+ years of deep experience in global steel structure supply, we leverage Shenzhen strategic location and China manufacturing advantages to provide one-stop steel structure engineering solutions for overseas clients.

Our business spans more than 60 countries, and we have become a highly reputable steel structure supply and service brand in emerging markets including Southeast Asia, Latin America, the Middle East, and Africa.

We pioneered a full-service export model: clients provide their land dimensions, and ZhongSai completes drawing planning, structural design, production and processing, complete material supply, standardized packing and container loading, customs declaration and shipping, and on-site technical guidance for installation — we do NOT perform on-site construction or installation — truly achieving "one place commission, global delivery."

Brand Evolution

Our Heritage: 15+ Years Industry Experience, 15+ Years Export

A family-owned steel structure enterprise with 15+ years of industry expertise — from a Dongguan engineering company to a global export brand.

2004 — Industry Origins

Founder Enters Steel Structure Industry

Mr. Yan Xizheng began working in the steel structure industry in 2004, accumulating 15+ years of hands-on experience in design, fabrication, project management, and international trade. This deep industry expertise forms the foundation of ZhongSai Steel Structure.

2011-2021 — Dongguan Engineering Era Deregistered

Dongguan Zhongsai Steel Structure Engineering Co., Ltd.

Registered in 2011 in Dongguan, Guangdong. Legal representative: Mr. Yan Xizheng. Registered capital: 8.88 million RMB. Operated for 10 years, building direct export experience. The company was deregistered on August 6, 2021, when business was restructured. Appears in Google search and third-party trade databases (e.g., Tendata) as historical records.

2023-Present — Dongguan Manufacturing Active

Dongguan Zhongsai Steel Structure Co., Ltd.

Established October 27, 2023 in Dongguan as the restructured manufacturing entity. Legal representative: Mr. Yan Zhikai (father of Yan Xizheng, family business continuity). Currently active and operating. This entity manages the Dongguan and Huizhou production bases (100,000 sqm total, 100,000 tons annual capacity), fabricating all steel structure products for export.

2024-Present — Shenzhen Global Trade Active

Shenzhen ZhongSai Steel Structure Import & Export Co., Ltd.

Established in 2024 in Shenzhen to centralize all international sales, marketing, and customer service. Legal representative: Mr. Yan Xizheng (same as original Dongguan company). Same family ownership, same factories (Dongguan + Huizhou), same engineering team — now with a dedicated global trade entity and new digital presence at zhongsai-steelstructure.com. Exported to 60+ countries.

Entity Clarification: ZhongSai Steel Structure (中赛钢构) is a family-owned business. The original company, Dongguan Zhongsai Steel Structure Engineering Co., Ltd.
