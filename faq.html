Frequently Asked Questions About Steel Structure Export from China

Expert answers to the most common questions about importing custom steel structure buildings from China

1 What are the standard sea packaging methods for custom steel structure buildings from China?

Steel structure components from China are packaged using three standard methods for sea freight: 1) Loose loading for large steel beams and columns (up to 12m length), secured with steel wire ropes and wooden spacers; 2) Bundle packaging for smaller components like purlins, bracing, and bolts, wrapped in waterproof plastic and steel bands; 3) Palletized packaging for accessories and hardware in wooden crates. All components are labeled with part numbers matching erection drawings. 40HQ containers are standard for most projects, with typical loading of 25-30 tons per container depending on component dimensions.

2 What certifications are required for steel structure export to Southeast Asia, Africa, and the Middle East?

Required certifications vary by region: Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Malaysia, Indonesia) typically accepts ISO 9001 quality management certification and GB standard steel mill test reports (MTR). Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa) often requires SONCAP (Nigeria), PVOC (Kenya), or COC certificates, plus ISO 9001. Middle East (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar) requires SASO (Saudi) or ESMA (UAE) certification, and sometimes CE marking for certain components. ZhongSai Steel Structure provides ISO 9001:2015, CIDB, and full material test reports (MTR) for all shipments. We can assist with region-specific certification applications as needed.

3 What is the typical production lead time for a custom steel structure warehouse or workshop from China?

Standard production lead time is 30-45 days after drawing confirmation and deposit receipt. The timeline breaks down as: 5-7 days for detailed structural design and drawing confirmation; 20-30 days for steel fabrication (cutting, drilling, welding, sandblasting, painting); 3-5 days for quality inspection and packaging; 2-3 days for container loading and customs declaration. Larger projects (500+ tons) may require 45-60 days. Rush orders can be accommodated with expedited production schedules at additional cost. Sea freight adds 15-35 days depending on destination port.

4 How is the price of a custom steel structure building calculated from China?

Steel structure pricing is based on total steel weight in metric tons, typically ranging from $1,200-$1,800 per ton FOB China for standard buildings. The final price depends on: 1) Steel quantity (tons) calculated from structural drawings; 2) Steel grade (Q235B vs Q355B); 3) Coating system (regular paint vs galvanized vs fire-resistant coating); 4) Building complexity (span, height, crane requirements); 5) Accessory materials (sandwich panels, doors, windows, gutters). To get an accurate quote, provide building dimensions (length x width x eave height), local wind/snow loads, intended use, and any special requirements. ZhongSai provides free quotations within 24-48 hours.

5 Do Chinese steel structure manufacturers provide installation guidance for overseas projects?

Yes, professional Chinese manufacturers like ZhongSai Steel Structure provide comprehensive installation guidance for overseas projects, but do NOT perform on-site construction. Our guidance includes: 1) Detailed erection drawings with part numbers and assembly sequences; 2) Step-by-step installation manuals in English with illustrations; 3) Video tutorials for key assembly procedures; 4) Remote technical support via WhatsApp/WeChat/email during installation; 5) Optional engineer dispatch to your site for on-site guidance and training (client covers flights, accommodation, and daily allowance). Typical installation teams of 5-8 workers can erect 200-500 sqm per day for standard buildings. We recommend hiring local steel erectors or general contractors for the actual installation work.

6 What steel grades and standards are used by Chinese steel structure manufacturers for export?

Chinese steel structure manufacturers primarily use GB (Chinese National Standard) steel grades: Q235B (equivalent to ASTM A36, S235JR) for secondary components; Q355B (equivalent to ASTM A572 Grade 50, S355JR) for primary structural members. For export projects, we can also supply materials certified to ASTM A36/A572, EN 10025 S235/S355, JIS G3101 SS400, or AS 3678 Grade 250/350 standards upon request. All steel comes with mill test reports (MTR) certifying chemical composition and mechanical properties. Welding follows AWS D1.1 or GB 50661 standards. Surface treatment includes sandblasting (Sa2.5), epoxy zinc-rich primer, and polyurethane finish paint. Galvanization is available for corrosion-prone environments.

7 What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom steel structure buildings from China?

There is no strict minimum order quantity for custom steel structure projects from China. ZhongSai Steel Structure accepts orders as small as 50 square meters (single garage/shed) up to 50,000+ square meters (industrial complexes). However, for economic efficiency, projects under 10 tons may have higher per-unit costs due to fixed design and setup expenses. Small projects are typically shipped via LCL (less than container load) or combined with other shipments. Standard 20ft containers hold approximately 15-20 tons of steel components. We recommend projects of at least 100 sqm for cost-effective procurement, but are happy to quote any size project based on your specific requirements.

8 How do Chinese steel structure manufacturers ensure quality control for export orders?

Quality control in Chinese steel structure factories follows a multi-stage process: 1) Raw material inspection - verifying steel mill test reports (MTR), checking material certificates, and dimensional verification; 2) Cutting and drilling inspection - CNC precision cutting with tolerance ±1mm, hole position verification; 3) Welding inspection - visual inspection, ultrasonic testing (UT) for critical welds, magnetic particle testing (MT) as required; 4) Surface treatment inspection - sandblasting roughness test (Sa2.5), dry film thickness (DFT) measurement for paint (typically 80-120 microns); 5) Final pre-shipment inspection - full dimensional check, part number labeling verification, packaging inspection. Third-party inspection by SGS, BV, TUV, or Intertek is available upon client request. ZhongSai provides quality inspection reports and photos for every shipment.

9 Can Chinese steel structure manufacturers design buildings according to local building codes?

Yes, experienced Chinese manufacturers can design steel structures according to various international building codes. We regularly design for: ASCE 7 (American Society of Civil Engineers) for wind and snow loads; AISC 360 (American Institute of Steel Construction) for structural steel design; Eurocode 3 (EN 1993) for European projects; AS 4100 (Australian Standard); IS 800 (Indian Standard); GB 50017 (Chinese Standard) as default. For local code compliance, provide your project location, local wind speed, snow load, seismic zone, and any specific code requirements. Our engineering team will design accordingly and provide structural calculation books for local building authority approval. We have successfully delivered projects complying with codes in over 60 countries.

10 What payment terms are standard for steel structure export from China?

Standard payment terms for steel structure export from China are: 30% deposit by T/T (telegraphic transfer) upon contract signing and drawing confirmation; 70% balance by T/T before shipment, or against copy of Bill of Lading (B/L) for established clients. Alternative payment methods include: L/C (Letter of Credit) at sight for larger orders; 50% deposit / 50% balance for special projects; Installment payments for very large projects (negotiable). Trade assurance through Alibaba is available for added security. We do not accept 100% after delivery terms for first-time clients due to manufacturing costs and material expenses. All prices are quoted FOB (Free On Board) Shenzhen or Guangzhou port, with CIF (Cost, Insurance, Freight) available upon request. Production begins only after deposit receipt and drawing confirmation.
